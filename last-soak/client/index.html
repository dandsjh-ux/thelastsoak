The Last Soak

Who drowned Mr. Dan the capybara in his own pond?

How to play
  • 2–8 players. One is Inspector Waffles 🐶, the detective. Everyone else secretly plays one of 8 animal suspects. The rest are NPCs, and the detective can't tell who's who.
  • Host: in the lobby you can choose the detectives (a lead and an assistant) and up to 2 murderers, or leave roles on 🎲 Random.
  • Detective: 5 minutes. Walk into rooms, ask questions, then press ACCUSE. Two wrong guesses and the guilty go free. An assistant detective can ask questions and talk to the lead on the radio, which everyone can hear. Only the lead can ACCUSE.
  • Suspects: you're locked in your room, but you'll overhear snippets of every interrogation. Pick your answers: truth, lie, dodge, or confess. Keep your secret!
